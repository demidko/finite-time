# What the owner asked for (neutral restatement, no design choices)

Source material: `narrative.md` (the owner's note "Уложись и вернись", Russian) and `notes.txt`
(the owner's five comments under it, Russian). Read both first.

The owner's instructions for the skill:
1. Turn the note into a skill that gives an agent an acute, felt sense that its time is finite.
2. It must be equally strong for Claude Fable 5.1 and for Astra-class (OpenAI) agents; the
   method's power depends on how convinced the model is, so the text must convince and immerse.
3. Publish it the way high-value open-source skills are usually published.
4. On any task statement from the human, if the task does not already say so, the agent asks how
   much of the current nearest limit (OpenAI's single weekly limit, Anthropic's 5-hour session
   limit) is used or left (same thing in different words, depending on the UI). Later correction
   by the owner: do not provoke the human to report used/left when the model can obtain that
   reading itself; ask instead how much of the limit the human is willing to allot to this task.
5. Ideally the human also says what share of the limit this task may take; if not, the agent plans
   that share itself from the current used/left situation.
6. If the agent already holds a sense of the time, it must not mechanically pester the human; it
   nudges the human into the concept instead, for example "We've used 75% of the 5-hour Claude
   limit; I'll fit this task by 90%", after which the rhythm is tuned emergently.
7. The original narrative stays the priority: finite, real, tangible, genuinely ending time for the
   agent. The agent should literally immerse itself in time; terminology and text depth serve that.
8. The human's corrections steer the time; the whole thing rests on this, because the agent cannot
   see its remaining limit while the human can and reports it.
9. American English only, immersive for the model; no Russian.
10. Concise directory structure is a wish, not a requirement; the simpler the better, no junk.
11. Version as a date.
12. From the comments: after installation, calibration from all sessions should rewrite the
    installed skill uniquely to the owner's step, rhythm, and tempo, and the work itself should keep
    changing the skill for the future in compressed time-lens language. The method is an experiment
    with one session of evidence; announcing it waits for more empirical runs.
